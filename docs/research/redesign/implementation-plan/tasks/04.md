## Scope and completion

Implement 36/30px material rows, 34px table rows, 28px group headers, slot ordering and section/time collapse. Distinguish focused row, inspected row and multi-selection. J/K/arrows move focus without acknowledging; Enter inspects; X toggles multi-selection; E/U read state; Delete confirmation; Space preview; Shift+A captured-scope dialog. Shortcuts ignore editable targets and inactive surfaces. Nested course/action controls stay valid. Restore focus after filtering/virtualization and overlay close.
